# Roadmap and open threads

Ideas raised during prototyping that aren't built yet, roughly grouped. Nothing here is committed.

## Story and arc
- An overarching arc in the RimWorld storyteller / Dispatch style: events paced by a director, not just random rolls.
- Something inside the black hole that needs warping or reaching; the exotic matter and horizon observations are the first hooks.
- Corporate saboteurs and lawsuits.
- Earlier concept: the station starts unmanned and gets staffed up over time.

## People
- A speech researcher for vocal and sapient specimens (expendable, in Meridian's eyes).
- Xenoanthropologists for sapient worlds and ruins.
- Leads who become cocky when constantly favored, and a threshold of pulls before they walk.
- Maverick replacement leads arriving by shuttle.
- Administrator walkthroughs that report up to you, with consequences for what they find.

## Research paths and funding
- Distinct market paths for results: military (best pay, ethically costly), biotech (lower but stable), industrial and manufacturing.
- Aborted experiments lose their investment; equipment upkeep degrades labs over time.
- Military contracts making dangerous work on staff more acceptable to Meridian, and more costly to morale.

## Labs and station
- An engineering or mechanics lab: containment upgrades, remote handling rigs, repairs.
- Volatile physics projects that can wreck a lab and force a rebuild or ejection.
- Multi-floor labs or a central cylindrical containment column for environmental studies, as the station grows.
- Lab layouts that change with the kind of experiment and the conditions it needs.

## Specimens
- The 15-specimen catalog from the design doc (Claude Docs) as hand-authored anchors alongside procedural generation.
- Hybrids with their own behavior traits and ethical fallout.
- Specimen sounds shaped by the home world's atmosphere (sound speed is already modeled).

## UI
- Dialog boxes with emotive portraits for all findings (partly built).
- Touch-friendly drag and drop in the planner (mouse works; touch untested).
